🌲Welcome to Hearthvale

Build a cozy riverside town and watch it come to life.
• Pick a building and click the ground — a villager will come build it.
• Cottages bring villagers; farms & fishing feed them; workshops earn coin.
• Growing the colony unlocks new buildings — and winter hearths burn wood!
• Place workshops beside their supplier (bakery by a windmill…) for a ⚡ bonus.
• The seasons matter — reap a big harvest in autumn, and stock food before winter.
• Keep folk happy (wells, taverns, a festival) — a merry town works harder & grows faster.
• Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · click a villager to meet them.
The intended balance — a fair start.
