📊 Town Almanac

🏝️ Voxel Isle

A tiny floating island, all yours. Grow a garden in the sky.

  • Drag to orbit · scroll / pinch to zoom · Q / E rotate
  • ⚒️ Till grass into soil, 🌱 plant a seed, 💧 water it (grows 2× faster)
  • Tap a ripe plant (it sparkles) to harvest it for coins & XP
  • 🧱 Build with blocks & decorations · ⛏️ Dig to remove
  • Level up to unlock new seeds, decor — and new floating islets
  • Moonpetals only grow at night. The stars are good for something. 🌙
  • 🎣 Tap the pond to fish — reel in 🐟🐠🦀 for coins (and goods for your stockpile); it freezes over in winter
  • Seasons turn every 3 days — spring speeds growth, winter slows it and brings snow ❄️
  • 🐦‍⬛ Crows land on growing crops and peck growth away — tap to shoo them, or post a 🎃 scarecrow to guard the plots around it
  • 🧺 Flotsam crates bob up in the pond — tap to crack them open (coins, sometimes a good) before they sink; a ⚓ Pier doubles the traffic and the haul
  • A 🗼 Lighthouse (level 8) shows the isle to the shipping lanes: passing freighters heave 📦 cargo crates overboard — triple the coin and always carrying a good
  • The 🔭 Observatory (level 9) charts the night sky: ✨ stardust falls twice as often and every wish surges the crops twice as hard
  • A 🌦️ Weather Vane (level 6) reads the sky: every shower is called half a minute out, and rain lingers 25% longer over the isle
  • 🎈 Balloon Tours (level 10) lift sightseers over the isle every few minutes — tips scale with every building standing (grounded in rain and winter)
  • A 🕊️ Dovecote (level 7) receives mainland letters — small goods orders at a 75% premium; tap the banner to send the dove home heavy (90s window)
  • The 🏮 Winter Market (level 8) only opens in the snow — skaters cross the ice every couple of minutes, paying 12 🪙 +3 per villager, turning the dead season into a working one
  • The 🛖 Ice Hut (level 8) sits out on the hard water — ice fishing opens the frozen pond: Arctic Char, Burbot and the pale 🌙 Moon Koi at a premium, all logged in the Angler’s Log
  • The ⛴️ Ferry Landing (level 6) brings day-trippers in the green seasons — the ferry docks every couple of minutes and the visitors tip 8 🪙 +2 per decor piece (capped at 40): the prettier the isle, the fatter the tips
  • The 🍁 Sugar Shack (level 7) taps the maples — spring boils every couple of minutes pay 12 🪙, and a boil in the rain runs double: every season now has its own earner
  • The 🏛️ Isle Museum (level 9) keeps a curator’s cabinet — the first of every species you land is mounted for a 30 🪙 finder’s bounty, and the winter table counts: eight mounts fill the cabinet
  • The 🦉 Owl Roost (level 8) works the night shift — after dark the owl hunts every couple of minutes for 9 🪙, and a wet night pays double: the balloon has the day, the owl has the dark
  • The 🐖 Truffle Pig (level 9) works the after-rain — every shower that passes, she roots the wet ground for a 20 🪙 truffle: the weather itself now pays out
  • The 🌽 Corn Crib (level 8) buys peace with the crows — a raider that fills up at the crib pecks less than half as hard (0.15× a crop's growth instead of 0.35×), everywhere on the isle, forever
  • The 🫙 Preserve Shed (level 8) jars the quiet days — one preserve a day, four on the shelf, and every seasonal festival the crowd buys the whole shelf at 12 🪙 a jar: patience, sold at a premium
  • The 🎲 Cloud Wager (needs the 🌦️ vane) — tap the chip to stake 30 🪙 on rain inside 90 seconds, paying 60: the vane calls showers half a minute early, so a called sky is the whole edge
  • The 🛟 Assessor's Policy — 25 🪙 insures the fields till dawn: a landed crow peck pays 50 in damages, a quiet day lapses the premium — the scarecrow and the crib are for prevention, the policy is for the day they miss
  • The 📝 Trader's Note (needs the Market) — 60 🪙 now against 75 of future trade: half of every Market sale and Tavern meal rides the note till it clears — farm income and tips stay whole, the debt is a lien on trade alone
  • ✨ Stardust falls from the night sky — tap the glowing shard before dawn; three shards make a wish and the whole garden surges
  • 🌸 In-season crops — each seed has a favoured season (shown by its icon in the seed picker); plant it then and it grows 1.5× faster and sells for +40%
  • Press F (or 👁️) for first person — WASD walk, mouse look, click to use your tool, F/Esc to exit
  • Hire 🧑‍🌾 villagers (LV 3) — a crew that roams doing whatever’s needed: harvest, collect goods, water & tend animals. Build the Tavern to give them roles (tap a villager)
  • 🐮 Livestock — buy a baby, raise it, then tap to sell for a lump sum, or keep it: grown animals drop 🥚🍄🧶🥛🧀 goods on the grass to collect. Keep a pair and they’ll have babies ❤️
  • 🐈 Companions — a cat (with the Tavern), a coin-digging dog (with the Farmhouse), and a duck on the pond come to live on your isle — each has a name; tap to say hello. Greet the cat five times and it starts bringing you little gifts
  • 🏘️ Town — buy land expansions to raise new islets, then buildings: Tavern (roles), Market, Barn (more animals), Farmhouse (faster crops)
  • 🏪 Trader’s Market — once built, animal goods 🥚🧶 and your harvested crops 🌾 pile into a stockpile (top-left) that a roaming Trader wagon drives in to buy in bulk; tap the Market to sell on the spot
  • 🍺 Tavern — besides unlocking roles, it cooks & serves your stockpiled goods for a steady stream of coins (a bigger crew keeps it busier); when it rains ☔, the villagers duck inside to wait out the shower
  • 📈 Market demand — each day the town wants a different good; sell or serve that good while it’s hot for +50% (shown in the stockpile bar)
  • 🥖 Bakery — takes 🌾 crops from your stockpile and bakes them into 🍞 bread worth ~3×, which the Trader then buys / the Tavern serves at the higher value; add a 🌬️ Windmill to bake faster & yield an extra loaf
  • 💧 Well — keeps the tilled soil around it watered on its own, so nearby crops grow at the 2× rate hands-free
  • 🪴 Greenhouse — crops keep growing at full speed through winter instead of slowing to a crawl
  • 🐝 Apiary — a beehive whose bees make 🍯 honey on their own into your stockpile (a valuable good the Trader buys & the Tavern serves), and pollinate nearby crops so they grow faster — build it beside your fields
  • 🍶 Brewery — ferments 🍯 honey from your stockpile into 🍶 mead worth ~2.5× (the honey chain’s answer to the Bakery), which the Trader buys & the Tavern serves at the higher value
  • A growing town — every building you raise draws a free settler to move in, and off-duty villagers wander over to the Tavern for a break 🍺
  • Seasonal festivals — when the season turns, your town throws a 🎉 festival with a coin bonus that grows with your villagers & buildings, and for a little while afterward all goods sell for +35% — a great time to cash in your stockpile
  • Town ranks — as you add buildings, villagers, land & wealth your settlement climbs from 🏛️ Homestead → Hamlet → Village → Township → Sky City, each with a coin gift (rank shown atop the Town panel)
  • Keys 1–5 pick tools · M mute · P photo mode · 🏆 achievements · autosaves
