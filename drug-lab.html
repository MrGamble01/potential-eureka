🌱 RUNNING THE CORNER
A grow in the back of a garage. Turn it into something bigger before the block turns on you.
• The loop is grow → harvest → sell. Plants ripen on their own. Walk up to a ripe one, carry it to the trim table, stash the bags, and sell to the buyers who come to you. Cash buys rooms, gear and people.
• The strain board is the cut in the tent. Garage Kush is free and even. The other cuts trade speed, price and heat, and the line on that board is what the next bag pays.
• The alley is today's neighbors. The same three marks for everyone, and any friend code you paste stays on this device. Pass their lifetime take.
• On a phone, use the stick and the big button. The button runs the next step — harvest, trim, stash, sell — so you never have to tap a tiny pot.
• Nia watches the door. Ask her and she walks the next step with you. Harvest once and she will call Raid Night — live through Sergeant Voss and the Night Lot out back is yours.
• Heat is the whole game. Every sale adds heat — 6 per unit before upgrades, and selling five at once costs five times as much as selling one. It bleeds off slowly on its own.
• At 95 heat the raid comes; at 100 you are busted and the run is over. A captain on the take will offer you a way out on the way up — once per approach.
• Cool it down on purpose. Lookouts, cleanup gear and the laundromat out front all drain heat, and they are the difference between a run that ends and one that compounds.
• Your stash holds 15 bags until you buy more room. Product past the cap is product you cannot sell.
• 📊 Watch the street price. Demand drifts between 0.75× and 1.35× and sometimes surges or crashes. Same bags, very different money — timing is a real decision.
• A bust is not the end. Earn enough and it forges a new identity: permanently better street prices on every run after.
